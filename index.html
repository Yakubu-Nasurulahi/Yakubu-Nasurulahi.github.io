Ask our team ↗
Which service is right for me?+

Start with the challenge you want to solve. Explore the service descriptions above or contact us with your goals, timeline and the support you need. We can discuss the best starting point together.

Can I enquire without choosing a school?+

Yes. Select “No — please help me find suitable schools” in the study enquiry form. Your academic background, interests, preferred countries and budget will help us explore suitable options with you.

What should I prepare for a study enquiry?+

Have your qualification, course interests, preferred intake and an idea of your funding ready. You can indicate which documents you already have. Passport numbers, financial records and document uploads are not needed for this initial enquiry.

Does submitting an enquiry secure admission or funding?+

An enquiry starts a conversation about your options. Admission, scholarships and visa decisions are made by the relevant institutions and authorities, and requirements depend on the programme and destination.

How does the website enquiry form send my information?+

The form prepares a summary in your browser. After reviewing it, open it in your email app or copy it into an email to info@syfroedge.com. You must send that email to complete your enquiry. Entries are not submitted to a website database.
